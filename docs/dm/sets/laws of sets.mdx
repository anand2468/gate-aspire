| law  | conditions |
| --- | ----|
| Idempotent law | $ A \cup A = A \\ A \cap A = A $ |
| Commutative law | $ A \cup B = B \cup A \\ A \cap B = B \cap A $ |
| Associative law | $ (A \cup B) \cup C = A \cup (B \cup C ) \\ (A \cap B) \cap C = A \cap (B \cap C ) $ |
| Distributive law | $ A \cup (B \cap C) = ( A \cup B) \cap ( A \cup C) \\ A \cap (B \cup C) = ( A \cap B) \cup ( A \cap C) $ |
| DeMorgan's law | $ ( A \cup B )^c = A^c \cap B^c \\ ( A \cap B )^c = A^c \cup B^c $|
| identity law | $ A \cup \emptyset = A \\ A \cap \emptyset = \emptyset \\ A \cup U = U \\ A \cap U = A $|
| compliment law | $ A \cup A^c = U \\ A \cap A^c = \emptyset \\ U^c = \emptyset \\ \emptyset^c = U $|
| involution law | $( A^c)^c = A $|
